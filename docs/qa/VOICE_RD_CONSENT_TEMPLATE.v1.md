# Voice R&D Consent Template v1 — DRAFT

**NOT APPROVED FOR REAL SPEAKER OR CUSTOMER COLLECTION. FOUNDER APPROVAL REQUIRED.**

This draft exists only to make the engineering workflow explicit. Before collecting real-person audio, Fonely must approve exact terms for:

- who may participate and how consent is evidenced;
- permitted R&D, commercial, model-training and derivative-model uses;
- external STT/TTS/provider transfer;
- compensation;
- retention, deletion, withdrawal and backup handling;
- access controls and reviewer roles;
- de-identification and PII/health-information handling;
- minors and background speakers;
- voice-cloning prohibition or permission;
- redistribution and licensing.

V1 engineering collection mode permits synthetic fixtures and internal synthetic-loopback testing only. It must contain no real patient, clinic, phone, address or health record.
