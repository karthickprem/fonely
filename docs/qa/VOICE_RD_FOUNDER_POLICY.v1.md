# Fonely Voice R&D Founder Self-Recording Policy v1

Karthick may record his own voice reading fictional Chennai Tamil/Tanglish dental scenarios for:

1. Fonely internal evaluation and quality measurement.
2. Benchmarking with Sarvam, Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI only.
3. Internal Fonely model training, fine-tuning, distillation, and error analysis.

## Restrictions

- No real patient, customer, phone, address, clinic record, or private medical information.
- Karthick must be the only audible speaker and an adult.
- No background speakers or minors.
- Voice cloning, speaker imitation products, redistribution, and sublicensing are prohibited.
- Access is limited to Karthick and explicitly authorized Fonely voice-R&D reviewers.
- A new provider, purpose, retention period, voice-cloning use, or redistribution requires a new policy version and consent.

## Retention and deletion

- Raw source audio is retained for up to 90 days unless renewed under a new approved policy.
- Deletion requests are allowed and remove the recording from active manifests and storage according to the deletion workflow.
- Provider transfers and completed model-training influence may not be technically reversible. Deleting source audio cannot guarantee removal of learned influence from an already-trained model.
- Minimal non-audio audit tombstones may be retained to evidence deletion.

This policy applies only to the founder self-recording studio and does not approve customer, patient, public, employee, contractor, design-partner, or minor collection.
